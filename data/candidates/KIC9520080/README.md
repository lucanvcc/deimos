# KIC 9520080

Withheld. I am not listing this one as a candidate, and no figure is published for it. It is not confirmed, and I do not claim it is new.

## The signal

- Period 2.377000 d, a search value with no refit.
- Depth 1578 ppm in the search. In the held-back data it is 1509 +/- 118 ppm (SNR 12.8). Same telescope and processing, so this shows the dip repeats, not that it is independent confirmation.
- No catalogue match within 65 arcsec. The community follow-up archive and the literature were not checked.

## Why it is withheld

- A faint companion (G 20.88) 3.47 arcsec away could not be tested by the second pixel test. The pixel tool could not decide in 12 of 12 quarters, and in Q10 the best fit is on that companion.
- KIC 9520089 (G 13.93), 19.2 arcsec away, was not modelled.
- A brightening of 2.3 to 4.3 sigma at phase 0.5 is unexplained.
- I have no bound on dilution, so I state no radius.
- The transit shape and star density are unmeasured, and the duration is a search-grid value, not a fit.
